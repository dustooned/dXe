The look, in three rules

1. White on black, 1-bit pixel art and dither; hard edges, no soft gradients, no glows. 2. The screen is 390 × 844; draw at 2× (780 × 1688) unless a row says otherwise. 3. Replace, don't rename: drop real art at the listed path. Sizes and templates: docs/ASSET_SIZES.md, docs/ART_GUIDE.md, docs/CHARACTER_ART.md, docs/BATTLE_ART.pdf.

NPC character states

Each NPC: 192 × 192, 1-bit look (black pixel art on white), GIF or PNG. Per docs/CHARACTER_ART.md. Talk and wait pieces for a feeling the NPC never shows in a battle are marked optional.
