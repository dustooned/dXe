Battle Art Brief

What to draw for each NPC in a battle, and exactly when it shows up.

The short version
  1. Draw each NPC at 192 × 192 pixels: black pixel art on a white background, like the Therapist, Deborah and Samun files you sent.
  2. Each NPC is a checklist of poses, with their own section. Work in waves: wave 1 puts them on screen, wave 2 makes the talk feel alive, wave 3 is their full range.
  3. Mouths are shut in every pose except the talk loops. They only move while the NPC's quoted words are being said.
  4. Send GIFs named Profile_<Npc>_<State>.gif (the exact names are on each NPC's page).

3 of 62 priority pieces are drawn across the four NPCs. The numbers in this brief come straight from the game's script: if a feeling never appears in an NPC's battle, you won't be asked to draw it.

How it plays

A battle, beat by beat

A battle is a conversation. The NPC says something, you pick a feeling and answer, they react, then speak again. Their face changes with each step:

1They are quiet
The resting face, held still. Nothing moves.
idle
2They speak
The mouth loops while their quoted words type out, and closes the moment the quote ends.
talk or talk_<feeling>
3Your turn
They wait for you, feeling what they feel: a short anticipation that plays once and holds its last frame.
wait_<feeling>
4You answer
A moment before they speak again, they take your words: a one-time reaction, then back to rest.
react_truth react_lie react_hit react_warm
5They close up (later)
When you miss what they were reaching for.
react_cold
6The ending (later)
You stay in touch, or you are pushed away. During their story, five images are shown in silence.
connect pushaway trauma_1…5

Rules that matter

  • The mouth only moves in talk poses. Idle, wait and react frames all have a shut mouth. Narration, stage directions and pauses never move it.
  • The talk loop starts on the idle picture. Frame 0 of every talk loop is the idle face, so when they start speaking the mouth begins exactly where it was resting.
  • Every frame gets its full time. 100 ms a frame by default (10 per second). If you export a GIF with your own frame delays, the game keeps them exactly.
  • Wait and react play once and hold their last frame. A single still is fine for any of them; the game just holds it.
  • Anything not drawn yet falls back to something that is: a missing feeling pose uses the plain talk or idle. So partial sets always work, and you can send in any order.
  • Living idle (optional). The idle can be a short loop (a glance, a blink). It rests on frame 0 and plays now and then, so the screen stays calm. Samun's placeholder works this way.
  • Wearing a mask. Some of Samun's and Rick's lines show a feeling on their face that isn't what they feel underneath. You draw the face they show.

The eight feelings

Happy Trust Fear Surprise Sadness Disgust Anger Anxiety

The colors are the game's own feeling colors, for orientation only. The art stays black and white.

Sending it

Sending the art

FormatGIF. One frame is a still, several frames are an animation. 192 × 192 pixels (a whole multiple like 1920 × 1920 is also fine if you draw big; we check it shrinks cleanly).
LookBlack pixel art on white, no antialiasing, no glow or blur. (The existing files also use one near-black ink for dithering, which is fine.)
NamesProfile_<Npc>_<State>.gif with the NPC and state capitalised: Profile_Deborah_Talk.gif, Profile_Samun_Wait_Anger.gif, Profile_Rick_React_Hit.gif. The exact names for every piece are on the NPC pages.
Timing100 ms a frame unless you choose otherwise. Loops for talk, one-shots for wait and react: the game decides, you just draw the frames in order.
WhereThe shared Drive: Chapter 1 / NPCs / <Npc>. Drop new files in the NPC's folder (make it if it is not there yet); the same name again means "replace".
Odd namesDon't worry about them. If a file is named differently (like Profile_Deborah01.gif), we map it by hand. Just tell us what it is.
