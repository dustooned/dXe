=== therapist_02
PROMPT: "One more, then I'll let you go." A beat. "What happened in the {color:Anticipation}dream{/color}?"

-- TRUTH
SAY: I don't know. Water. Something underneath it. I couldn't breathe.
REACT: A pause. Then, quieter: "Thank you for telling me that. Most people give me the short version." A soft click, a chart opening on her end. "See the box in the corner? It stays light when you don't have to remember what you told people."
EFFECTS: integrity+2 lucidity+2 stability-1 trust+2
DEBT: 0
NEXT: (end)

-- LIE
SAY: Nothing, really. I don't remember.
REACT: "Mm-hm." A pause just a beat too long. "We can leave it there for today." Another soft click. "You'll notice the box in the corner keeps count. It's fine. Everyone carries some."
EFFECTS: integrity-1 stability+1 trust-1
DEBT: +1
TAGS: Denial
LEDGER: You told your therapist you didn't remember the dream.
NEXT: (end)
